The troll blocked the bridge. "Answer my riddle, or pay the toll! What has four legs in the morning, two at noon, and three at night?"

"A human," said the knight.

"Correct! Three gold, please."

"But I answered your riddle!"

"That was the riddle fee. The bridge fee is separate."
