Gnorman the gnome arrived at the royal ball completely naked.

The king gasped. "Where are your clothes?"

"At the dry cleaner," said Gnorman. "They said they'd be ready by six."

The king glanced at the clock. "It's midnight!"

Gnorman nodded. "Terrible service. And they charged me for the extra hours."
